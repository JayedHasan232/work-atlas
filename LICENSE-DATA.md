## What counts as data and text

- Occupation records: names, aliases, codes, field values, descriptions, and their revision history.
- Field definitions, scale anchors, prompts and JSON schemas in `data/`.
- Open data releases and the content returned by the public API.
- Documentation in `docs/` and the other Markdown files in this repository.

## What is not covered

- **Third-party data** keeps its own license. Every value records its sources, and each source records
  its license. Examples: the O\*NET Database (CC BY 4.0, attributed with its version number) and
  ILO Working Paper 140 (CC BY 4.0). CC BY material may be combined into CC BY-SA data.
- **Private submissions.** Individual income and experience submissions are never part of the open
  data. Only aggregates built from a minimum number of submissions are published.
- **The name and logo.** The license does not grant use of the Work Atlas name or logo to suggest an
  official or endorsed project. See [BRAND.md](BRAND.md).

## How to attribute

When you reuse Work Atlas data or text, give credit like this:

> Work Atlas contributors, *Work Atlas (কাজ)*, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Link to the occupation
> page or to the data release you used, and say if you changed anything.

For an open data release, also name the release version or date. If you share an adapted version, you
must share it under CC BY-SA 4.0 or a compatible license.

## Contributing data and text

By contributing data or text — through a pull request or, later, through the website — you agree to
license it under CC BY-SA 4.0. Pull requests also need a DCO sign-off (see
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
